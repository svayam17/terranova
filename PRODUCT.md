## Platform

## Users

B2B buyers of finishing materials for premium projects:

- Architects and interior designers, who carry the design intent and specify materials, finishes and references.
- Real estate developers and commercial project owners, who own the BOQ, quantities, CapEx and commercial viability.
- Hospitality groups and hotel operators.
- Procurement heads.
- Project management consultants (PMCs) and project teams, who hold the technical specification, quality and performance parameters.

They arrive with a requirement (a product, a specification, a BOQ, a drawing, a reference or simply a project need) and need to judge quickly whether Terra Nova can be trusted to source it.

## Product Purpose

Terra Nova (TerraNova Finishing Materials Pvt. Ltd.) is a global luxury materials sourcing and procurement studio. It sources, develops, manufactures, imports and supplies finishing materials for premium architectural, hospitality, commercial and real-estate projects. The website exists to communicate credibility, capability and trust to B2B buyers and to turn a project requirement into an enquiry.

Success: a visitor understands within seconds that Terra Nova is an established international sourcing house, sees its project associations and material range, understands the procurement journey, and starts an enquiry.

## Positioning

"We don't sell what we have. We source what the project needs."

Requirement first, sourcing second: Terra Nova starts from the project requirement and works backwards to the product, manufacturing partner, country of origin and procurement route, rather than selling from inventory. It balances three project priorities: design intent, technical requirements and project economics.

## Operating Context

- Buyers bring a BOQ, specification, drawing, product reference, design requirement or a project need.
- The procurement journey: requirement, global sourcing, OEM and custom manufacturing, factory and supplier evaluation, production and quality control, international procurement and import, logistics and project delivery, project execution and installation.
- Engagement can be one stage or the complete procurement cycle.

## Product Principles

1. Requirement first: every surface starts from what the project needs, not from a catalogue.
2. Prove with real associations and the real procurement journey, never with invented claims.
3. Speak to three stakeholders at once: design intent, technical requirements, project economics.
4. Make the next step easy: any requirement, however partial, can start an enquiry.
